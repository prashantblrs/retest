How a retest hit is built

A mechanical port of the Tab 5 demand-zone method — the same rule on daily, weekly and monthly bars. As of Mon Nov 16, 2026 close.

Zone, impulse & retest

  • Base = 1–5 small-bodied candles (body < 50% of range) immediately before the impulse. Zone = base wick low to the highest base body top.
  • Impulse = 1–2 bullish candles, range ≥ 2× ATR(14), body ≥ 60% of range; within 5 bars a close ≥ base high + 3× ATR that also breaks the prior 20-bar swing high.
  • Volume gate (hard): the impulse candle’s volume ≥ 2.0× its 20-day average — no exceptions. (Tab 5 only flagged 1.3×.)
  • Retest = the latest bar’s low inside the zone, or its close within 2% above the zone high, after a pullback of ≥ 8% from the first-leg swing high.
  • Zones older than 252 daily / 104 weekly / 60 monthly bars are ignored, and a zone is discarded once any bar closes below its low. Fresh = never traded back into; Multi-test = touched before and held.

Price, stop, target & R:R

  • Price = the latest bar’s close on that timeframe — the figure the stop, target and R:R are computed from. It can differ from the daily close on weekly and monthly rows.
  • Stop = zone low × 0.99. Target = the first-leg swing high after the impulse.
  • R:R is blank (—) where the target has already been crossed — the swing high sits below the retest price.
  • Reversal flags a confirming candle on the retest bar (e.g. bullish engulfing, or a close in the top 30% of the bar’s range).
  • Scan coverage: see the summary in the scan data. Floors are price ≥ $5 and 20-day average daily volume ≥ 500,000 shares. Monthly detection uses completed bars through Oct 2026; the latest bar (November, Nov 1–16 sessions) is the retest bar only. Sector is blank (—) where no sector mapping exists for the name.

Read this before the levels

  • Survivorship bias: the universe is currently-listed top-2,000 US stocks only. Delisted names and past members that failed are absent, which flatters any hit-rate reading of these results.
  • Zones are indicative levels from a mechanical rule, not trade calls. Verify each hit on the TradingView chart before acting. Research tool only, not investment advice.
